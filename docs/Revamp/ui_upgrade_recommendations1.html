What's wrong & what needs to change

The app has solid functionality, but its UI is fighting the "Fresh Mint" visual direction you defined. Here's the full diagnostic and the transformation roadmap.

Current Problems
  • Dark navy buttons feel heavy and dated — no connection to "Fresh Mint"
  • Plain white backgrounds with zero surface layering or depth
  • System default typography (no brand voice)
  • Generic step dots in App Tour (asterisk + circles)
  • No iconography to give the app visual language
  • No gradient, shadow or elevation cues on cards
  • Form fields use plain 1px grey borders
  • Settings screen looks like a stock Android preference screen
  • Color palette is inconsistent (purple buttons, navy buttons, grey cards)
Proposed Direction
  • Mint green (#3DD68C) as the primary brand color — buttons, highlights, CTAs
  • Warm near-black (#1C2333) for surfaces in dark accents, dialogs, and headers
  • Subtle mint tint (#F0FDF7) on white backgrounds to stay on-theme
  • Sora or Nunito as display + body font for a friendly, modern feel
  • Animated pill-style progress indicator in App Tour
  • Icon-backed group cards with gradient avatars
  • Dark-surface Insights tab for chart contrast
  • Focused input fields with mint glow ring on focus
  • Gradient primary buttons with branded shadow
