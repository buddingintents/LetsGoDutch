Short version:

- New `Activity` tab for group create, join, expense, and settlement history
- Better insights and settlement flow polish
- Expense categories and notes now persist end-to-end
- Recent settlements now support both `Open` and `Share`
- Clearer reminder wording with explicit `They owe you` / `You owe them`
- PDF readability and overall UI polish improvements
